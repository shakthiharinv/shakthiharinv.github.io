Dailies

Info

The graduate Graphic Design Program at Maryland Institute College of Art boasts of hosting talented and leading designers from the industry as part of their guest lecture series. For a class project known as 'Dailies,' I created conceptual posters to promote these lectures. In total, eleven posters were crafted, each under the constraints of black and white color schemes and a one-hour time limit. The task was to encapsulate the essence of each designer's work through typography. I employed diverse techniques including paper cutouts, 3D lettering, pattern building, dry brush lettering, and water washing to achieve this goal.
