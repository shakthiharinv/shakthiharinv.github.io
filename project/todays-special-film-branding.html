Today’s Special

Info

Today's Special is a funny movie about a Chef who inherits his family's Indian restaurant. It's full of kitchen accidents and celebrates a love for cooking. I chose it for my project because it has comedy, romance, philosophy, politics, and FOOD! The title was crafted to capture the comedic essence, while the diverse selection of colors mirrors the vibrant array of Indian spices featured prominently throughout the film by the key characters. Final deliverables encompass the main poster, character posters, and promotional materials such as billboards, tote bags, and social media assets. Credits to the photography team of the movie and the respective actors.
