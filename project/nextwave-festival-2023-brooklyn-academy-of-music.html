Nextwave Festival 2023

Info

Nextwave is an annual festival comprising of opera, theater, music, and dance hosted by the Brooklyn Academy of Art(BAM), NY. The program had performances that are genre-busting, formally daring, and thought-provoking. I designed the identity for the festival and chose to build a modular system using movable rectangular forms and an unconventional grid system that celebrated both the fresh and star talent, which was translated into both print and digital medium. Deliverables included printed posters, brochure, signs, digital billboards, flags, swags, and promotional material.
