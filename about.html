About

About

Shakthi is an art director, visual designer, and illustrator based in Sunnyvale, USA. A chemical engineer and a business technology analyst in the past, he is currently a design generalist engaged in exploring and leveraging graphic design to narrate impactful stories.

His areas of interest include typography, typeface design, AI as a design tool, illustration, web-design, marketing and brand design, meaningful user interactions, publication & print design, automation, documenting design processes, and productivity, among others.
